🎬 A personalized family movie experience

Relive Your Childhood Through Their Eyes.

Discover the movies that came out when you were your kid's age. Add your birthday and your kid's birthday, and this app finds the movies released right around the time you were exactly as old as they are now — so you can share the films you grew up with and relive a little bit of your own childhood together.

Just enter your birthday and your child's birthday.
︾

Why Parents Love Same Age Movies

Movies are more than entertainment — they're memories. Same Age Movies helps you rediscover the films that shaped your childhood and experience them again with your kids.

1

Enter two birthdays

Your birthday and your kid's birthday are used to build a personalized movie timeline.

2

Travel back in time

We'll surface the movies that were in theaters when you were exactly the age your kid is today.

3

Share the experience

Watch together and pass along the stories only you can tell about your favorite childhood movies.

Imagine This...

Your kid just turned 8.

When you were 8 years old, The Lion King had just hit theaters. Instead of endlessly scrolling for something to watch, you share one of the movies that made your own childhood unforgettable — and tell them what it was like the first time you saw it.

That's the idea behind Same Age Movies.
