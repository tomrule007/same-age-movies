# Same Age Movies
​A simple web app that helps you find movies that were released when you were the same age your kids are now. Relive your childhood by sharing your favorite classics with the next generation.

## ​How to use
1. ​Enter your birthday.
​2. Enter your child's birthday.
3. ​The app will calculate the year you were that age and suggest movies from that time!

* Birthdays/Alias's are only stored locally and never leave your device.
